A hospital built for our own people

I started Raj Hospital because families here were losing precious hours travelling to the city for ordinary treatment. Since around 2012, my brother and I have kept one rule: nobody is turned away at the door.

Many patients today are children of those we treated in our first years. That trust is why we keep the building clean, the staff patient and the charges explained.

Dr. R.K. Prasad, Founder
